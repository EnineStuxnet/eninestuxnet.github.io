Weight is set by the container, not the fluid — every line runs from 0.1u empty up toward its full weight at capacity, and stops there. The chart tops out at 1.7u; a few containers are heavier than that, so their line is dashed and cut off where it would cross the ceiling. Click a container to pin its line, or hover the chart to see what's under the cursor.
